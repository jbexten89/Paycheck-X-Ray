      1. Open a statement in the pay portal (Statement tab).

      3. Paste below. Several statements in one paste is fine.

      Everything stays in this browser on this device. Account and routing numbers are never read or kept. PDFs need a text layer, so use the portal's Print → Save as PDF. Photos are read on this device; the photo itself is never uploaded.
