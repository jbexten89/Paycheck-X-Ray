Total compensation

Ohio unemployment from your employer statements

One year per line: the "OH UT ER" amount from that year's total-compensation statement first, then any other Ohio employer items (OH Debt Repay, OH WorkCmp ER), e.g. "2015 27 + 105 + 152.77". Add employers together. Years you leave out are estimated from the nearest year's OH UT ER rate.

Base pay and raises

Raise is base pay at year end compared with the year before. Prices is the change in the yearly average Consumer Price Index. Real is the raise after inflation; green beat inflation, amber fell behind.

    Account balances

    Enter your balances

    One year per line: the year, your 401(k) balance at Dec 31, your HSA balance at Dec 31, what you spent from the HSA that year, and what you moved from HSA cash into investments that year. Write the HSA as cash/invested to split it. For this year, use balances as of today. Example: "2025 350000 8000/32000 1200 6000". Leave off numbers you don't have; "2025 350000 40000" works too.

        Add stubs

        1. Open a statement in the pay portal (Statement tab).
        2. Press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
        3. Paste below. Several statements in one paste is fine.

        Everything stays in this browser on this device. Account and routing numbers are never read or kept. PDFs need a text layer, so use the portal's Print → Save as PDF. Photos are read on this device; the photo itself is never uploaded.
